Research experience

2023 – 2026

Identifiability and nested-model comparison in latent variable models

Quantitative Psychology Lab, York University · Dr. Xijuan (Cathy) Zhang

Compared competing confirmatory factor models for balanced Likert-scale data, testing nesting and equivalence relations in R. Evaluated identification rules for factor models with correlated errors and cross-loadings, using Wald rank tests on simulated and empirical data. Third author on the resulting methods paper.

2024 – 2025

Sensitivity of RMSEAD to baseline-model misspecification

SEM Lab, UBC · Dr. Victoria Savalei

Studied how a misspecified baseline model distorts tests of measurement invariance in multi-group factor models. Built an R Shiny app that shows RMSEAD across one- and two-factor models under user-chosen misspecifications such as residual covariances and cross-loadings.

2022 – 2024

Cross-cultural variation in healthy sleep duration

Culture and Self Lab, UBC · Dr. Steven Heine

Fit multilevel models to large cross-national survey data, separating within- and between-country effects of chronotype and sleep duration on health, including cross-level interactions with country sleep norms. Fourth author on the published article in PNAS.
