About me

I build statistical models for noisy human data: latent-variable and Bayesian models for measurement, and time-series models for how emotions change from one moment to the next. I am now carrying that toolkit into machine learning and deep learning, with mental health as the application I care about most.

BA, double major in Statistics and Psychology (UBC, 2026). Visiting student at Peking University, Fall 2026. Applying to PhD programs in statistics and biostatistics for Fall 2027.

Directions I want to explore in graduate school:

  • Bayesian and probabilistic machine learning. Models that learn from data and report how sure they are: Bayesian inference, scalable computation such as HMC and variational methods, and probabilistic deep learning.
  • Uncertainty and evaluation of ML and LLMs. Measuring what models actually do: calibrated uncertainty, careful validation, and ideas from measurement theory applied to evaluating machine learning systems and large language models.
  • Methods for health and behavioral data. Intensive longitudinal data such as experience sampling and wearable sensors, with mental health as the application I care about most.
